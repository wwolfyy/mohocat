**Owner's call, 2026-08-09**, made while seven other records were re-classified to the new
`deferred` status (`R-0411`). `R-0140` (`BACKLOG.md`'s B2) and `R-0142` (B5) were not among them.

**Why they are different:** both wait on an **owner decision** rather than on a dependency. The
`deferred` test asks whether you can name the condition that would restart the work — and when
that condition is "the owner decides", the item is actionable by the very person who reads the
open list. Parking it would hide it from the one reader who can unblock it.

📌 This is the sharper edge of the `deferred`/`open` line: not everything blocked is deferred.
Blocked on a thing is deferred; blocked on a choice the reader can make is open.

⚠️ **The related correction, recorded on `R-0411`:** `R-0139` and `R-0141` had been imported as
`open` without that call being flagged, while the `[-]` mapping was flagged. By `BACKLOG.md`'s own
definition — "known gaps… deliberately not fixed at the time, no date" — the unflagged `open`
reading was the worse distortion of the two.
