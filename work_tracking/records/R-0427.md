**Deferred on a named condition: it starts when the owner archives `docs/planning/**` to
`docs/archive/`** — the by-hand move `R-0407` settled. There is nothing to do before then, and
doing it early would break links that currently resolve.

**Why it is not optional when that day comes.** The 20 companion-document records
(`R-0327`…`R-0346`) are the one importer that copied no prose: each is a pointer, and its
`detail_ref` points **outside `work_tracking/`** at a path under `docs/planning/`. Moving those
folders breaks **all 20 at once**, and a pointer record whose pointer is dead carries nothing at
all.

⚠️ **They must be repointed in the same change as the move**, not after it. This is the same rule
as "move an item and delete its origin in the same change" — a link that is broken between two
commits is a link somebody reads in between.

📌 **The content stays recoverable either way**, because each record's `source_ref` pins a commit.
That is a safety net, not a substitute: `git show` recovers the file, but only after the reader
has worked out that the link is stale and why.
