# R-0406 — Companion planning documents are indexed as one pointer row each, never flattened into rows

> **decision · done · adopted** · 2026-08-08
> Migrated from `work_tracking/MIGRATION_JOB.md` — "Decisions made during execution"
> (`work_tracking/MIGRATION_JOB.md#D-index-companion-docs@44e08fe`).

---

**A 787-line plan is a document, not a row.** The companion plans under `docs/planning/` are not
work items that happen to be long; they are documents with structure, and breaking them into
registry rows would have produced a lossy summary of each while destroying the thing that made
them useful.

**What was done instead:** one record per document, with `detail_ref` pointing straight at the
file where it already lives. Nothing was moved, rewritten or copied into `records/`.
`companion-docs.js` is the only importer in the migration that copies no prose.

**The consequence to know about:** those 20 `detail_ref`s point outside `work_tracking/`, at
`docs/planning/**`. When the owner archives those folders by hand (`R-0407`), all 20 links go
stale in that change — `R-0427` carries the repointing. The `source_ref` pins mean the content
stays recoverable either way, but the links will not fix themselves.

📌 The status of each record came from **the document's own header, not its folder**, which
mattered: two plans sitting in `completed/` were still headed as plans, and one sitting in
`pending/` is genuinely not started.
