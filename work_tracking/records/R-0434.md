# R-0434 — Path-based tenancy is not a one-way door — the host-rewrite middleware and the `domains` config stay

> **decision · done · adopted** · 2026-07-28 · §9
> Lifted from `docs/planning/pending/tenancy-url-model-decision-20260728.md` §10

**Adopting path-based tenancy (`R-0395`) does not delete the host-based mechanism.** The
host-rewrite middleware stays, and the `domains` arrays stay in `mountains.json` even though
they become vestigial in normal operation.

🔑 **Why keep them: a future mountain that genuinely needs its own hostname can be given one** —
accepting the multi-origin costs **for that tenant only**, as a deliberate opt-in rather than as
the platform default. The costs are known and recorded: the per-origin sign-out defect
(`R-0396`), the login friction and the credential it would take to close it (`R-0433`), the
per-subdomain Firebase authorized-domain chore, and the DNS/Vercel/TLS ritual per mountain.

**It costs nothing to leave in place.** The middleware already exists and is an adapter over the
`[mountain]` route segment the app tree is built on; keeping it is the absence of a deletion, not
a maintained feature.

📌 **This is what makes the URL-model decision reversible in practice rather than only on paper.**
The argument that could have reversed it was branding — whether a second mountain's owner needs
`manisan.mohocats.org` rather than `mohocats.org/manisan` to agree to participate. The owner
answered "a path is fine" on 2026-07-28, but that answer is about the mountains in view today,
not about every future partner.

⚠️ **Do not "clean up" the `domains` arrays or the host-rewrite path as dead code.** They read as
unused under path-based tenancy, and removing them is what would turn a reversible decision into
a one-way door.
