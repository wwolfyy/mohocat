📌 **A split always writes a new revision of the parent too** (`SCHEMA.md` §5). If the children
cover the parent entirely, the parent goes `status: done`, `outcome: superseded`, with a `note`
naming why; if they are partial, the parent keeps its status and the new revision exists purely to
record the break-out — which is exactly why `note` is mandatory on an otherwise-unchanged
revision. Check out the parent as well as the child, or check-in will reject the reference.
