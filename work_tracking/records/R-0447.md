# R-0447 — Seven places in AGENTS.md and WORKFLOW.md explained less than they appeared to

> **change · done** · 2026-08-12
> Owner-raised. The owner said my written explanations skipped steps, quoted a passage back, and
> then asked whether the documents written on 2026-08-10 had the same problem. They did.

---

**The review was of my own prose, against four failures the owner's examples exposed:** a causal
chain compressed into a subordinate clause; a term coined during the work and never defined; a
conclusion stated before the step that produced it; and a short closing line standing where the
explanation should have been rather than after it.

## The two that mattered most

🔴 **`AGENTS.md` R4 contained the exact defect the owner had just objected to in conversation.** It
read _"otherwise it lives in prose nobody queries"_, which depends on a fact the file never states:
**a query returns rows, and never returns the prose inside a record's body.** Without that sentence
a reader has no reason to believe the claim, so the rule looked like a stylistic preference. It now
states the fact first. ⚠️ **This is worth noticing as a pattern rather than a slip** — the same
compression appeared in conversation and in the file that every session loads, within a day, which
suggests it is a default rather than an accident.

🔴 **R2 used a distinction that only existed in the other file.** _"Only the row is versioned"_ has
nothing to contrast against unless you already know a record is a row plus a separate prose file,
and that is introduced in `WORKFLOW.md` §2.2. R2 now names both parts, and says which one
`checkin.js` versions.

## A flourish that was also inaccurate

📌 **`WORKFLOW.md` §2.1 ended _"a log you can go back and rewrite is not evidence of anything."_**
That is both a substitute for the explanation and an overstatement: an editable log would still be
evidence of the current state. 🔑 **The narrower claim is the true one and is checkable** — if rows
could be edited in place, a row would only tell you what someone believes now, and could no longer
be read as what was decided when it was written. That is what it says now.

## The remaining four

- **`WORKFLOW.md` §2.2 opened on "the 'not before' half of the same-change rule"** — a phrase coined
  in `AGENTS.md` R4, met cold by anyone arriving at §2.2. Replaced by naming R4 and R2 and stating
  how they divide.
- **"and it bites in a specific way"** — removed. The following sentence already did that work.
- **"a better teacher than a line you read a week earlier"** — asserted a claim instead of showing
  it. Replaced with what actually happens: the rejection prints
  `checkout.js --id R-0154` at the moment you need it.
- **"rather than a tidier editing habit"** — argued against a position nobody held. Removed, leaving
  the real reason: a `note` on a revision is the only place a judgement call gets written down.

## Found during the review, and fixed with it

⏭️ **`R-0443` still said there were three rules.** It named R1, R2 and R3, which was correct on
2026-08-10, and `R-0444` added R4 the same day without leaving a pointer back. A reader who reached
`R-0443` first would have believed the set was complete. It now carries a forward pointer, and its
heading no longer counts. 📌 **Records are point-in-time, so its text was not false** — what was
missing was the link forward, which is the part a later reader cannot reconstruct.

⚠️ **Marker and bold density was deliberately left alone**, and is a separate discussion with the
owner. In `AGENTS.md` lines 63–96 there are roughly twelve emoji markers across thirty-four lines,
and the repository uses those markers as signals — a warning triangle for a watch-out, a key for the
load-bearing point. At one every three lines they stop separating anything. 🔑 **But thinning them
here alone would start a second house style**, inconsistent with the rest of `AGENTS.md` and with
every older record, so it is a decision about the repository rather than about this week's text.
