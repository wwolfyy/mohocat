# R-0405 — Work tracking lives in a root `work_tracking/` folder, scripts included — not under the app's `scripts/`

> **decision · done · adopted** · 2026-08-08
> Migrated from `work_tracking/MIGRATION_JOB.md` — "Decisions made during execution"

**Owner's call, 2026-08-08.** Everything to do with work tracking — the two design documents, the
schema, the registry files, the `records/` prose and the scripts — lives under a single root
folder, `work_tracking/`.

**Why:** work tracking is not application code. The app's `scripts/` folder holds build and
migration scripts that ship with the product; these do not. Keeping them apart means the
work-tracking tooling has no share in the app's dependencies, its lint configuration, or its CI
job — which is what makes `R-0418` (an independent CI job on a different Node version) a
one-line change rather than an argument with the app's build.

📌 It also means the whole subsystem can be read, reviewed, or removed in one place, rather than
being scattered across `docs/`, `log/` and `scripts/` the way the artifacts it replaces were.
