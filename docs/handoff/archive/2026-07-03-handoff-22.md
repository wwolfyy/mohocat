## 6. Gotchas / notes

- **Off-brand vs on-brand is the whole distinction.** Off-brand (blue/yellow) buttons cause
  double-work and were eliminated. On-brand hand-rolled gradient buttons are visually final
  and don't — don't treat them as a mobile prerequisite.
- **Semantic colors are intentional.** Green "sent" boxes, ⚠️ warning cards, `ui/Alert`
  variants, and the Kakao `#FEE500` vendor button are **kept on purpose** — don't "brand" them.
- **Next dev HMR flakiness.** Rapid multi-file edits corrupted the `/login` route into
  transient 404s (Fast-Refresh full reloads); a **clean dev restart** fixed it (tsc/smoke
  green, curl 200 on a fresh server). If you see a public route 404 during a restyle session,
  restart dev before chasing a phantom bug.
- **The canonical input focus pattern** is `focus:outline-none focus:ring-2
focus:ring-brand-300` (`ui/Input` + 동참 form). A bare `focus:ring-brand-300` (no width /
  no `outline-none`) is inert — the native blue outline shows instead.
- **Login routes:** both `/login` (`src/app/login/page.tsx`) and `/pages/login` exist; the
  butler clients redirect to `/pages/login`, the nav login button links to `/login`.
